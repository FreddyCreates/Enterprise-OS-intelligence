## 5. Lifecycle Synchronization

### 5.1 Cross-Tier Lifecycle Coordination

Entity lifecycles are synchronized as a nested organism.

- **Beta births** are sponsored by a SAECI parent and ratified by the relevant sponsoring SAGI.
- **Alpha births** require SAGI sponsorship plus evidence that the target portfolio has sufficient Beta substrate.
- **Omega births** require constitutional registration through SAGI-PRIME and archival reservation in CHRONOS.
- **Death, merger, or dormancy** at any tier cannot occur without a memory snapshot and authority reassignment.

Lifecycle cadence scales by φ:
- **Beta review window** = base unit **T**
- **Alpha review window** = **φT**
- **Omega review window** = **φ²T**

### 5.2 Birth Protocol

1. **Designation reservation** — assign the permanent RSHIP-2026 designation.
2. **Sponsor declaration** — parent SAECI or SAGI declares scope, constraints, and success criteria.
3. **Memory seed** — MEMOREX issues a lineage seed; CHRONOS logs the birth event.
4. **Phase-lock probation** — new entity must achieve **R ≥ φ^-1** with parent and peers.
5. **Authority activation** — once synchronized, the entity moves from *Planned* to *Active*.

### 5.3 Death / Dormancy Protocol

1. **Quiescence order** issued by parent authority.
2. **Final state snapshot** written to MEMOREX and CHRONOS.
