# RISK

## Risk principle

Risk control is part of the infrastructure.

## Required gates

- drawdown gate
- volatility gate
- exposure gate
- leverage gate
- slippage gate
- counterparty gate
- chain / smart contract gate
- exchange failure gate
- kill-switch gate

## Minimum rules

- no unbounded leverage
- no untracked live promotion
- no missing execution receipts
- no position size outside mandate
- no bypass of blocked risk results

## Kill-switch doctrine

Triggers may include:

- loss threshold
- abnormal volatility
- exchange or chain failure
- agent malfunction
- execution frequency anomaly
- unauthorized action
- human override
