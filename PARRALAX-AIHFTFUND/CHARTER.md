## Agent authority doctrine

No agent receives unlimited authority.

Suggested authority ladder:

- Level 0 — Observer
- Level 1 — Analyst
- Level 2 — Simulator
- Level 3 — Proposer
- Level 4 — Guarded Executor
- Level 5 — Governed Operator
- Level 6 — Restricted Sovereign Agent

Authority expansion requires governance records.

## Execution doctrine

```text
Market Signal
-> Signal Agent
-> Signal Score
-> Risk Agent
-> Strategy Protocol
-> Trade Authorization
-> Execution Agent
-> Venue/Broker/Chain
-> Trade Receipt
-> Portfolio Update
-> Memory Writeback
```

Signals do not equal trades.

Trades require authorization.

Authorization requires risk clearance.

Execution requires receipts.

Receipts become memory.

## Risk doctrine

Risk is part of the core infrastructure, not an add-on.

Rules:

- no unlimited execution
- no hidden routing
- no treasury movement without receipt
- no live upgrade without governance record
- no fake performance reporting
- no silent kill-switch override

## Asset issuance doctrine

No internal asset without:

- purpose
- supply logic
- rights model
- transfer rules
- governance status
- proof record

## Governance doctrine

Governance exists to preserve bounded authority over:

- agents
- strategies
- treasury
- protocols
- tokens
- NFTs
- risk limits
- live execution upgrades

No meaningful governance action should occur without a governance record.

## Compliance principle

Build sovereign infrastructure, but do not confuse sovereignty with legal invisibility.

Legal, market, custody, and compliance boundaries matter from the start.
